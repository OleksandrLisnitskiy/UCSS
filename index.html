Supporting our community, together.

UCSS is dedicated to helping those who are unable to help themselves. As a non-profit charitable organization, UCSS responds to the needs and interests of the Ukrainian community and individuals through a wide range of services.

Community care

Practical support for newcomers, seniors, families, and people in need.

UCSS Edmonton connects people with settlement guidance, immigration information, translation help, community referrals, and support for vulnerable community members.

Our services are grounded in Ukrainian community values while remaining open and welcoming to those who need assistance.

What UCSS helps with

Clear next steps when life in a new place feels complicated.

Newcomer connection

Meet-and-greet events and settlement referrals help newcomers find community and services.

Information access

UCSS shares timely support information and helps community members find the right programs.
