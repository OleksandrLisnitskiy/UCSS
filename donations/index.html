Every fall, Ukrainian Canadian Social Services – Edmonton turns to the community for support through its annual fundraising campaign. UCSS activities are guided by the needs of our community in Edmonton and the surrounding area.

UCSS helps newcomers, seniors, and community members facing difficulties or hardship. Our services are free of charge and available to those who need them.

Our daily operations rely on the generosity of individuals who understand the importance of providing support and services to vulnerable members of our community in Edmonton and the surrounding area. Your contribution helps us continue making a meaningful difference in the lives of those we serve.

For all donations $10 and more income tax receipts will be issued

Thank you for your donations!
